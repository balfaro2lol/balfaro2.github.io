About Me!

Hello! My name is Briana Alfaro and I am currently a Junior Animation Major at Arizona State University. For many years, I have loved stories from little comics I've seen in the newpapaers to feature animated films to even evesdropped conversations, courtesy of my mom's gossip.

While I haven't had much professional experience so far, I have done my fair share of personal and collaborative projects! From projects made from my university's clubs to in-school assignments and even my own personal works, I love the art of storyboarding and 2D animation.

You can click here to see some of my projects, both from within to outside of my classes.!
